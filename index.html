Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access the meeting room?

Upon successful payment and booking, you will receive a unique digital PIN code via email/WhatsApp. This PIN will activate the smart lock on the boardroom door exactly 15 minutes before your scheduled slot.

Is outside catering allowed for full-day rentals?

Yes. While we provide a basic tea/coffee pantry station, you are welcome to arrange outside food (like Swiggy/Zomato). However, to protect the premium furnishings, we request that heavy meals be consumed in the designated pantry area.

What is the cancellation policy?

You can reschedule or cancel your booking with a full refund up to 24 hours prior to your slot. Cancellations made within 24 hours are non-refundable to protect schedule integrity.
